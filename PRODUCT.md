# Splat2BIM product context

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

Researchers, technical reviewers, and portfolio visitors inspect seven construction
captures against LAS and BIM. The viewer supports date comparison, layer selection,
and structural diagnostics; the reports explain the evidence and its limits.

## Presentation scope

Use English for the viewer and current portfolio reports. Preserve the existing
Three.js/Spark interface, colors, controls, and interaction model. This is a
language update, with no new brand direction or visual redesign.

## Writing principles

- Use short, concrete control labels and consistent technical terms.
- Distinguish reference agreement from independent survey accuracy.
- Keep units, dates, sample counts, thresholds, and limitations explicit.
- Preserve historical numerical evidence and identify presentation-only changes.

## Accessibility

Translate accessible names, loading states, errors, and image descriptions as well
as visible labels. Retain keyboard focus styles, native form controls, and the
collapsible panel. Verify that English labels fit narrow and desktop layouts.
